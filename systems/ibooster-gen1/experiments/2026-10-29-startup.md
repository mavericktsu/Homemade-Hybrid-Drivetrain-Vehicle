# Startup and maintained ignition

Date: October 29, 2026. Evidence type: summary of recorded bench results.

## Question

Can the bench tool maintain communication and reach the readiness condition used by the Gen 1 community reference?

## Setup

Tesla donor 1045523-00-C, SH-C30A adapter, Windows, and the custom Python bench tool. The exact software commit is not recorded in this public starter. See [hardware context](../hardware.md).

## Observations

An early short feedback window was initially investigated as a communication problem. The owner then clarified that ignition had only been applied momentarily.

With maintained ignition, the working notes record 11,524 valid-CRC feedback frames and no CAN reports when inspected at 76.8 seconds. Reference status changed from 4 to 7 and stayed at 7; raw reference position stayed at 320.

A subsequent small-step experiment produced only a minor feedback change, and the owner reported no visible movement. A larger later sweep was manual operation and must not be counted as commanded motion.

## Interpretation

The maintained-ignition run supports handshake compatibility on this setup. It does not establish successful motion, physical calibration, or clutch operation.
